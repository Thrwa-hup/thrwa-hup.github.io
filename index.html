About Tharwa Publisher

Tharwa Publisher is the official publishing platform of Tharwa Youth Association, a non-profit in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. Program coordinators submit post requests; each request passes an automated check against the association's publishing policy and is reviewed and approved by the media team. Approved content is published to the association's official accounts through each platform's official API. For TikTok, the account owner reviews a preview, chooses the privacy level and settings, and confirms every post.
